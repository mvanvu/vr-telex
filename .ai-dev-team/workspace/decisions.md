D11 (E3) — ime-core word end: every flush commits `Composer::finish(ctx.last)` and stores its language in the context (`last: Lang`, no text). At focus_out, reset, mode toggle and set_enabled(false) the word ending at that event is finished with the context's language first, then `last` becomes Unknown (`flush_and_forget`); focus_in, context creation and the broken context of `recover` start Unknown. Boundary keys, COMMAND chords, EN-mode/password flushes, set_caps (edit-mode change, SEC-13), destroy and recover of other contexts keep `last` (they do not start a new run of words in the field). A MAX_WORD_KEYS overflow sets `last = En` after its flush (plan). The overflow flush commits what `finish` returns, like any word end: for a 32-key run the engine changed, that is the raw keys (AC-1), not the shown text the plan called "unchanged behaviour". Surrounding mode replaces the whole shown word (DeleteSurrounding of all its chars + Commit) only when the finished text differs; nothing otherwise.

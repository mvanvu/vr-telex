D13 (19:40) — Tone placement: modern convention. Tone on the vowel with a diacritic (â ă ê ô ơ ư; on ơ for ươ, on ê for iê/yê/uyê); oa/oe/uy → second vowel (hoà, khoẻ, thuý); ia/ua/ưa (no final consonant) → first (mía, múa, mưa); u after q and i after g are consonant parts (quý, giá) unless no other vowel (gì, gìn). The golden list is built from a public Vietnamese syllable list by a qc agent with its own reverse function (word → Telex keys), never by the engine's author.
